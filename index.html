Market Education

DAM

The Day-Ahead Market schedules energy for each operating hour ahead of real time. This website evaluates whether projected DAM pricing beats plant cost.

LBMP

Locational Based Marginal Pricing is the NYISO price at a location, zone, or generator bus. The right PTID/location matters because congestion and losses can move the price.

Generator Bus / PTID

The plant should use the most accurate generator-bus or settled pricing point available. Zone prices are useful for rough estimates, not final settlements.

Fuel Variable Cost

Gas price plus basis/transport multiplied by heat rate. This is the largest lever in most gas-fired margin decisions.

BPCG / Uplift

Bid Production Cost Guarantee and uplift can affect actual settlement. This simplified model includes a note field but does not calculate NYISO settlement uplift.

Min-Run / Min-Down

Suggested run blocks respect minimum run hours and simple startup allocation. Real-world commitment constraints may require more detailed modeling.

Startup / No-Load

Startup and no-load costs are allocated across selected run blocks to estimate net profitability and break-even LBMP.

Settlement Caveat

This is a decision-support forecast, not a settlement system. Actual NYISO settlements may differ because of bidding, dispatch, reserves, outages, deviations, uplift, and metering.
